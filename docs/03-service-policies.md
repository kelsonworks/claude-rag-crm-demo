# Service Policies

Policies for the fictional demo company Bluebird Heating & Air.

## Scheduling & Priority

- We book 2-hour arrival windows. Your technician texts you about 30 minutes
  before arrival.
- No-cool and no-heat calls received **before 2:00 pm** get same-day priority
  during peak season (June–August and December–February). Calls after 2:00 pm
  are scheduled for the next morning unless the emergency line is used.
- Comfort Club members always move to the front of the standard queue.

## Cancellations & Rescheduling

- Cancel or reschedule at no charge with at least **24 hours notice**.
- Inside 24 hours, a **$45 late-cancellation fee** applies. We waive this fee
  the first time for every customer — things happen.
- If we miss our arrival window without notifying you, you receive a $50 credit
  on your invoice.

## Warranties

- Repairs carry a **1-year labor warranty**.
- Installations carry a **2-year labor warranty**.
- Parts are covered by the manufacturer's warranty — typically 5 to 10 years
  when registered. We submit the registration on your behalf within 14 days of
  installation and email you the confirmation.

## Payment & Billing

- Payment is due on completion. We accept card, check, and ACH.
- Installations over $2,500 require a 30% deposit at scheduling.
- Deposits are fully refundable until equipment is ordered.
- Balances unpaid after 30 days accrue 1.5% per month.

## Refunds

- Comfort Club memberships can be cancelled anytime with a prorated refund for
  unused visits.
- If a repair fails within its labor-warranty window, we return and fix it at
  no charge — no re-diagnosis fee.

## Permits & Code Compliance

We pull permits for every system replacement and schedule the city inspection.
Permit costs are included in your quoted installation price, never added later.
