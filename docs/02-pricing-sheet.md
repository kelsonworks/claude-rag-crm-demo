# Residential Pricing Sheet (2026)

All prices are flat-rate and quoted before any work begins. Quotes are valid for
30 days. Prices below are for the fictional demo company Bluebird Heating & Air.

## Diagnostic & Service Call Fees

- Standard diagnostic visit (during office hours): **$89**. This covers travel,
  a full diagnosis of one system, and a written flat-rate repair quote.
- If you approve the quoted repair during the same visit, the $89 diagnostic fee
  is **credited toward the repair total** — you do not pay it twice.
- After-hours, Sunday, or holiday emergency dispatch: **$189 flat**, which
  includes the first 30 minutes on site. Repair work is quoted separately.
- Comfort Club members: one diagnostic fee waived per membership year, and
  members pay the standard $89 rate (not $189) for after-hours calls.

replacement includes the permit, city inspection scheduling, old-equipment
haul-away, and a 2-year labor warranty.

## Other Services

- Duct cleaning: **$429** for up to 12 supply vents, $18 per additional vent.
- Dryer vent cleaning: $139 standalone, or $89 when added to a duct cleaning.
- Media filter cabinet installed: $520 – $640.

## Financing

0% for 18 months on installations over $3,000, through a third-party lender,
subject to credit approval. Ask the office for the application link — we never
handle your credit information ourselves.
