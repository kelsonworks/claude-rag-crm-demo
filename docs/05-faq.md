# Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ for the fictional demo company Bluebird Heating & Air.

## How often should I replace my air filter?

Standard 1-inch filters: every 1–3 months, more often with pets or allergies.
4-inch media filters: every 6–12 months. Comfort Club Plus members receive
filters by mail so this never slips.

## Do you service my brand of equipment?

Yes — our technicians service all major residential brands. If your system uses
a proprietary part we do not stock, we can usually get it within 2–4 business
days from our regional supplier.

## What areas do you serve?

Crestwood, Ashford Hills, Pinehurst Valley, Lakemont, and Dover Plains —
roughly a 25-mile radius from our Crestwood shop. Outside that radius we charge
a $45 extended-travel fee, quoted up front.

## How fast can you get here in an emergency?

No-cool and no-heat calls received before 2:00 pm get same-day priority during
peak season. Overnight, the emergency line dispatches the on-call technician,
usually within 2–3 hours. After-hours dispatch is $189 flat (standard $89 for
Comfort Club members).

## Should I repair or replace my system?

Our rule of thumb: multiply the repair quote by the system's age in years. If
the result is over $5,000 — for example a $700 repair on a 9-year-old unit —
replacement usually makes more financial sense. Your technician will show you
both numbers and the math; we quote repairs and replacements separately so you
can decide without pressure.

## Do you offer financing?

Yes: 0% for 18 months on installations over $3,000, through a third-party
lender, subject to credit approval. See the Pricing Sheet → Financing.

## Do I need to be home during service?

For diagnostics, yes — the technician will want to show you what they found and
get your approval before any work. For tune-ups on member systems, we can work
from a lockbox or garage code if you have one on file with the office.

## What is SEER2 and why does it matter?

SEER2 is the current federal efficiency rating for air conditioners — higher is
more efficient. Moving from an older 10 SEER unit to a 15.2 SEER2 unit
typically cuts cooling energy use by roughly a third, though actual savings
depend on your home and usage.
