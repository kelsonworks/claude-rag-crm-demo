# Comfort Club Maintenance Plans

Membership plans for the fictional demo company Bluebird Heating & Air.
Both plans are month-to-month and can be cancelled anytime with a prorated
refund (see Service Policies → Refunds).

## Standard Plan — $16/month

Also available as $180/year paid annually.

- Two seasonal tune-ups per year: cooling system in spring, heating in fall.
- 15% off all repairs.
- Priority scheduling ahead of the standard queue.
- One diagnostic fee ($89) waived per membership year.
- After-hours emergency calls billed at the standard $89 rate instead of $189.
- We track the schedule and contact you when a tune-up is due.

## Plus Plan — $27/month

Also available as $300/year paid annually. Everything in Standard, plus:

- 20% off all repairs (instead of 15%).
- Filter delivery: up to four standard 1-inch filters per year, shipped to you.
- $150 per year of loyalty credit toward a future system replacement. Credit
  accrues for up to 5 years (maximum $750) and is applied to any replacement
  we install.
- One dryer vent cleaning included per year.

## What a Tune-Up Includes

Each tune-up is a 21-point checklist. Highlights: refrigerant pressure check,
capacitor and contactor test under load, condenser coil rinse, blower amp draw,
heat exchanger inspection, flue draft test, condensate drain flush, thermostat
calibration, and air filter replacement if you have one on hand.

## Fine Print

- Plans cover up to two systems at one address; each additional system is
  +$8/month.
- Repair discounts do not stack with other promotions.
- Loyalty credit has no cash value and is not transferable between addresses.
