# New Customer Onboarding — What to Expect

Onboarding guide for the fictional demo company Bluebird Heating & Air.

## Before Your First Visit

- You'll receive a confirmation text with your 2-hour arrival window the
  evening before, and a heads-up text about 30 minutes before the technician
  arrives, with their name and photo.
- Please clear about 3 feet of space around your indoor unit (furnace or air
  handler) and outdoor unit, and secure pets in another room.
- If you know where your air filters are located, that saves a few minutes —
  but the technician will find them either way.

## During the Visit

- The technician starts with a short walkthrough: what's the system doing, when
  did it start, any past repairs you know about.
- Diagnosis comes with photos or video of what was found — you see what we see.
- You receive a written flat-rate quote **before** any repair work begins.
  Nothing is started without your approval. The $89 diagnostic fee is credited
  toward the repair if you approve it during the same visit.

## After the Visit

- A digital invoice with before/after photos arrives by email the same day.
- For installations, we file the manufacturer warranty registration within 14
  days and email you the confirmation.
- We check in by text about 48 hours after a repair to confirm everything is
  running as it should.

## Your Service History

We keep the full service history for your address — every diagnosis, repair,
photo, and tune-up. Any technician who visits can pull it up, so you never have
to re-explain an old issue.
