# Bluebird Heating & Air — Company Overview

> Bluebird Heating & Air is a **fictional company** created for this demonstration.
> All names, numbers, and prices in this corpus are invented.

## Who We Are

Bluebird Heating & Air is a family-owned residential HVAC company founded in 2011,
serving the Crestwood metro area. We have 9 employees, including 5 state-licensed
service technicians and 2 install crews. We hold State Mechanical License
#MC-104882 (fictional) and carry $2M in general liability coverage. Every
technician is background-checked and EPA Section 608 certified.

## What We Do

- Repair and maintenance for residential air conditioners, heat pumps, gas
  furnaces, and ductless mini-splits (all major brands).
- Full system replacement and new installations, including permits and inspection.
- Duct cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, and indoor air quality upgrades.
- Light commercial service for rooftop units up to 7.5 tons.

We do not service boilers, oil furnaces, geothermal systems, or window units.
